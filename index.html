📋 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the Oracle identify my food triggers?
A: FartOracle uses a hybrid machine learning engine. It analyzes the specific acoustic frequency, amplitude, and waveform patterns of your digestive sounds (CoreML) and matches them against known dietary signatures.
Q: Is my audio data being recorded or uploaded?
A: Absolute not. All analysis happens in real-time, 100% on-device. Audio is processed for milliseconds and then instantly discarded. No sounds ever leave your phone.
Q: Can I change how the Oracle talks to me?
A: Yes! Head to "The Vault" tab. You can toggle between "Clinical" (strictly data-focused) and "Sarcastic" (full roasts) personas. The Oracle retroactively updates your entire history based on your choice.
Q: What is the "Ego Ceiling"?
A: The Oracle is humble. Because dietary science is complex, our AI caps confidence at 90%. Anything higher is statistically impossible, and the Oracle will likely roast you for trying to be "perfect."
Q: How do I purge my history?
A: If you need to clear the evidence, go to "The Vault" and tap "Purge History." This permanently shreds all local records from your device.

🔧 Troubleshooting

  • Oracle not hearing anything: Ensure you've granted Microphone permissions in iOS Settings > FartOracle.
  • Low Confidence Results: Ensure you are in a quiet environment. Background noise can interfere with the acoustic signature.
  • App unresponsive: Close and restart the app. Ensure your iOS version is up to date (iOS 17+ recommended).
  • Haptics not working: Check if "Vibrate on Results" is enabled in The Vault and that your phone isn't in "Low Power" or "Silent" mode.
  • Missing Food Icons: In Clinical mode, the Oracle uses DNA strand icons. Switch to Sarcastic mode in The Vault to see full food emojis.
